GUARDRAILS
- Products are preserved; no destructive product operation added.
- Neon remains authoritative.
- Outbox/realtime architecture is preserved.
- Valkey remains transient and non-authoritative.
- AI cannot run raw SQL.
- AI cannot approve its own requests.
- High-risk actions prepare plans; only approved recovery plans can execute in Stage 8.
- Critical financial/security/destructive operations are not executable by AI in Stage 8.
- AI provider is disabled by default.
- Governance and tool-policy versions are pinned on AI records.
- High-risk action plans expire after 24 hours.
- Final model output passes a deterministic protected-content guard.
- Rejected tool calls and output guardrail blocks are auditable.
- No provider secret is exposed to the browser.
